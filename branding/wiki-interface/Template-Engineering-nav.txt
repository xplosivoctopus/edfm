== Usage ==
Place near the bottom of any Engineering-related article (module,
blueprint, Experimental Effect, Engineer, or materials page) so a reader
never has to backtrack to the main [[Engineering]] hub just to reach a
sibling section. Fixed link set, no parameters — maintained in this one
template rather than repeated on every page, so renaming or adding a hub
later only means editing this page. See [[Elite Dangerous Field Manual:
Engineering Architecture]] for the full Engineering page architecture.

<pre>{{Engineering nav}}</pre>

Reuses [[Template:Related articles]]'s tile styling with a custom label
rather than introducing a second navigation component.
